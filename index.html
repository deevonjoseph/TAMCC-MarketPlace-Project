Contact, then decide

The platform hands the two students a conversation and stops there. Payment, delivery and identity checks stay outside the product.

Report is always visible

Every listing detail carries the report action with a fixed list of reasons, so a suspicious item reaches the moderator in one step.

Sold keeps its record

The page stays, the status changes, the contact button disappears. A marketplace that deletes sold items loses the history buyers rely on.

Errors sit with the field

The message appears under the field that failed and names the fix. Nothing pops up in the middle of the screen to interrupt typing.

Fixed lists, free text

Category, condition and reason are chosen, never typed. Description is free, because only the seller knows what they are selling.

Prices are positive

The validation rule in the design specification reads: price must be a positive number in XCD. This form shows the rule failing on purpose.

FR-06 · FR-07

Moderator queue

Reported listings wait here for a decision. A student reports, a moderator hides or restores, and every decision is written down.

Only a moderator account can open this screen. The reporter's name appears here and nowhere else.

One queue, no dashboard

The moderator gets a table and two buttons. Reports arrive with a reason, a count and an age, so the oldest problem is read first.

Every decision is written

Hiding or restoring writes a row with the account, the reason and the date. The audit trail is what lets the lecturer check the feature.

False reports are a risk

Any student can report, so a report could be used to hide a rival's listing. The register records this as risk R-11 with a response and a trigger.

FR-01

Sign in and register

Guests may read every listing. Publishing, contacting, reporting and moderating all require an account.

New here? Create an account with your name, email and a password of at least 8 characters.

Reading needs no account

A student who only wants to check a price never fills in a form. The account starts at the moment they want to act.

Only what the product needs

Name, email, password. No address, no payment details, no student record lookup. The password is stored as a hash.

Failed attempts are logged

The error names the problem and the session expires after inactivity, both taken from the input specification IN-01 and IN-02.
